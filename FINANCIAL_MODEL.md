## 1. Ground Realities of the AKGEC Campus

Understanding the exact physical constraints of AKGEC transforms Quest into a hyper-profitable campus utility:

### A. Strict Hostel In-Times (The Lock-In Effect)
* **Girls Hostel (GH)**: Closes at **6:00 – 6:30 PM**. Girls cannot leave the hostel building after 6:30 PM. Any printout, stationery, evening canteen snack, or gate delivery must be completed before 6:30 PM or brought to the hostel gate by peers.
* **1st-Year Boys Hostel**: Closes at **10:00 PM**. Strict junior curfew creates intense internal demand for gate drops and snacks between 8 PM and 10 PM.
* **Senior Boys Hostels (2nd, 3rd, 4th Year)**: Closes at **12:00 AM (Midnight)**.
* **Zero Night Canteen**: There is **no night canteen** inside AKGEC. Late-night food is either pre-ordered from outside gates (Zomato/Swiggy delivery dropped at Main Gate 1) or purchased before the day canteen/Nescafe closes.

## 6. How the Academic Micro-Gig Escrow Works in App

```
1. Requester Posts: "Need Compiler Design Lab Manual (12 pages), due Friday 9 AM"
   - Uploads photo of question sheet / syllabus.
   - Escrow Lock: Requester pays ₹140 into Quest wallet.
2. Peer Writer Claims: Writer accepts task in app.
3. Verification & Handover:
   - Writer delivers physical handwritten manual to requester's room door.
   - Requester verifies pages and enters 4-digit OTP.
4. Instant Payout:
   - Writer gets ₹100 instantly via UPI.
   - Quest keeps ₹40 in platform balance.
```
